Case 01

AI-Driven Content Engine

A GDPR-compliant, automated content workflow for cross-cultural audience engagement.

2025 · Orthodontic Gallery, London

The Context & Vision

Orthodontic Gallery needed to scale its multi-channel localized marketing across diverse demographics — without compromising clinical rigor, brand tone, or GDPR compliance. The goal wasn't just efficiency; it was building a content system that could think, adapt, and stay on-brand at scale.

The Architecture

Instead of manual translation and fragmented workflows, I architected a closed-loop AI content engine:

  • RAG & DAM Integration: Connected digital asset management with semantic retrieval to ensure brand voice and clinical accuracy across all output.
  • Automated Pipeline: Streamlined the end-to-end flow — from content generation, compliance review, and multi-channel distribution to performance analytics (GA4, Meta, CRM).

Impact at a Glance

-65% Content Production Time (equivalent to saving 2–3 FTEs) · Consistent Brand Expression · Reusable Knowledge Assets

Case 02

Scaling Public Trust: The Regional Nutrition Initiative

An integrated brand-building and trust campaign for a regional nutrition programme spanning 168 locations and multi-tier stakeholders.

Chongqing Yangle Catering, China

The Context & Vision

A large-scale project faced low initial awareness and complex stakeholder dynamics. The challenge wasn't just reaching people; it was building institutional and public trust across a fragmented ecosystem (schools, government, media, and parents).

The Strategic Approach

Built the programme's communication framework and social content system from the ground up:

  • Stakeholder-Centric Messaging: Tailored content strategies — bite-sized education for parents, official bulletins for schools, and impact stories for public PR.
  • Replicable Campaign Toolkit: Built standardized content workflows enabling synchronized execution across 168 locations while maintaining single-voice consistency.

Impact at a Glance

项目一

AI 内容引擎

一套 GDPR 合规的自动化内容工作流，服务于跨文化受众的内容触达。

2025 · Orthodontic Gallery，伦敦

背景与目标

Orthodontic Gallery 需要在多元文化并存的伦敦市场，规模化地推进多渠道本地化营销——同时保持临床内容的严谨性、品牌调性的一致性和 GDPR 合规要求。这个项目的目标不止于提效，而是要构建一个能够思考、适应、并在规模化过程中始终守住品牌调性的内容系统。

架构思路

摒弃人工翻译和碎片化的工作流，我设计了一套闭环的 AI 内容引擎：

  • RAG + DAM 融合：将数字资产管理接入语义检索能力，确保所有输出内容在品牌调性和临床准确性上保持统一。
  • 自动化流水线：打通从内容生成、合规审查、多渠道分发到效果分析（GA4、Meta、CRM）的端到端流程，同时实现内容的自动沉淀与可复用管理。

核心成果

内容生产时间缩短 65%（相当于节省 2–3 名全职人力）· 品牌表达一致性 · 可复用的知识资产

项目二

规模化公共信任：区域营养改善计划

面向 168 个业务点和多层级利益相关方的品牌建设与公共信任传播项目。

重庆阳乐餐饮管理，中国

背景与目标

一个覆盖学校、政府、媒体和家长等多方利益相关者的大型民生项目，初期公众认知度偏低。项目需要解决的不仅是“触达”的问题，而是在一个高度分散的生态中，建立机构公信力与公众信任。

策略路径

从零搭建项目的传播框架和社交内容体系：

  • 分受众内容策略：为家长定制轻量化的科普内容，为学校提供官方信息通报，为公众媒体输出项目成果与影响力故事。
  • 可复制的传播工具包：建立标准化的工作流，确保 168 个业务点在统一品牌表达的前提下同步执行传播动作。

核心成果

项目认知度从 10% 提升至 63% · 成为区域标杆 · 接待 10+ 同行机构交流学习 · 建立可复制模式并被其他区域项目采纳
